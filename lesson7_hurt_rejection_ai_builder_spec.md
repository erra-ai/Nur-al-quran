# Lesson 7 — "Hurt, Rejection, and Self-Worth" — AI Builder Specification

**Program:** Strong Heart, Wise Mind — Quranic Life Skills for Muslim Kids
**Age:** 10–13 · **Format:** standalone `lesson7.html` (iframe-loaded, same 8-section pattern as lessons 1–3)
**Core rule:** Your worth was set by Allah before anyone voted on it.

## Learning goal
A story-centered Islamic SEL lesson that teaches children what to do when exclusion and cold rejection hit — honor the hurt, anchor self-worth in Allah's words, and respond in a way that protects your dignity.
