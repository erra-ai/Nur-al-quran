# Lesson 11 — "Gratitude Without Denying Pain" — AI Builder Specification

**Program:** Strong Heart, Wise Mind — Quranic Life Skills for Muslim Kids
**Age:** 10–13 · **Format:** standalone `lesson11.html` (iframe-loaded, same 8-section pattern as lessons 1–3)
**Core rule:** Gratitude and grief can live in the same heart — neither deletes the other.
**Main tool:** Honest Shukr: Name the hurt — Name the gift — Thank — Act

## Learning goal
A story-centered Islamic SEL lesson that teaches children that saying alhamdulillah does not mean pretending everything is fine — real shukr holds the gift and the wound together, honestly.
