# Lesson 4 — "Fear and Worry" — AI Builder Specification

**Program:** Strong Heart, Wise Mind — Quranic Life Skills for Muslim Kids
**Age:** 10–13 · **Format:** standalone `lesson4.html` (iframe-loaded, same 8-section pattern)
**Core rule:** Fear is a signal, not a fact — dhikr is the shelter.
**Main tool:** The Anchor: Call to Allah — Check the thought — Slow the body — Make one move

## Learning goal
Teach children to stand with fear and worry: name the what-if loop, cool the body with dhikr/dua/wudu,
check whether the feared thing is certain, and take one small practical action.

## Sections
1. Main story: Amina's What-If Loop (exam anxiety, MCQ checkpoints inside)
2. Quran anchor: Ar-Ra'd 13:28 (hearts settle by dhikr) + Ali 'Imran 3:173 (hasbunallah) + balance card
3. Prophetic lens: the du'a of Yunus in the darkness + the Prophet's ﷺ regular refuge-dua against ham/hazan
4. Understand the difference — Signal / Unhelpful thought / Wise choice + do-not-teach warning
5. The Anchor tool (4 skill cards) + interactive Worry Scale (1–10)
6. Transfer practice: Bilal and the Dark (power cut)
7. Home challenge: My Worry-to-Du'a Switch
8. Parent/Teacher guide + Anchor ordering game + scenario checkpoint (Huda's speech)
